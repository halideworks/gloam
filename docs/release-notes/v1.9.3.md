# Gloam 1.9.3

- Keep an app's profile active while you work on another screen. In the profile's Advanced controls, enable **Apply while the app is running** and choose its target display.
- Find your Gloam version in the tray menu, Dashboard, and Game Lab.
- Gameplay Lock stays steady during background checks, and your usual display settings return when the app closes.

Thanks for the suggestion in [#9](https://github.com/halideworks/gloam/issues/9)!
